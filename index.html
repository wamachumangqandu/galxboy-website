Brief History

GALXBOY was created by Thatiso “Vuittots” Dube, a visionary designer from Mamelodi, Pretoria, South Africa, who grew up immersed in the vibrant energy of township life, where music, art, and self-expression were central to daily life. Initially aspiring to be a DJ or music producer, Thatiso discovered a passion for fashion inspired by his older brother. Using basic tools like Windows Paint and a cracked version of Photoshop, he began designing T-shirts for friends, which eventually led to the launch of his first brand, CHEESEBOY, during high school. CHEESEBOY later evolved into CHEESEGIRL to include female classmates, laying the foundation for GALXBOY.
